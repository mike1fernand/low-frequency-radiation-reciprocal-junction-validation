## An important correction to common wording

The baseline does contain a claimed rigorous infinite-lattice positive lower bound, not merely a finite-matrix tolerance calculation. But that theorem establishes a floor of10.37% over a specified±5%box, not that every value equals12.6% or that its displayed central decimals have a rigorous common error bar. Matching this standard for73.6% means distinguishing a central estimate, certified lower threshold and independent packet corroboration.
